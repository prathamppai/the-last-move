# 🎬 THE LAST MOVE
### A Short Film by Bablade Studios

> Every game has a final move. Every friendship has a breaking point.

## About the Film

**THE LAST MOVE** is a psychological thriller exploring friendship, betrayal, guilt and forgiveness.

Five friends share a common interest in paranormal activities. During an exploration of an abandoned building, they begin playing Jenga. However, what starts as an ordinary gathering takes an unexpected turn when Dev mysteriously disappears.

An argument over responsibility and safety tears the group apart. A year later, a mysterious message brings them together again, leading them back to the place where everything began.

But some games are never truly over.

## Genre

- Psychological Thriller
- Mystery
- Paranormal Drama

## Central Themes

- **Friendship:** The strength and fragility of human relationships.
- **Betrayal:** The consequences of feeling abandoned by those closest to you.
- **Guilt:** The burden of decisions made in moments of uncertainty.
- **Forgiveness:** Whether forgiveness can repair something that has already fallen apart.

## The Symbolism of Jenga

The Jenga tower is the central symbol of the film.

Each block represents a memory, a decision or a connection shared between friends.

As the tower becomes increasingly unstable, it reflects the gradual breakdown of their friendship.

The final move represents the moment when everything falls apart.

## The Story's Central Elements

**The Abandoned Building**

The location where everything begins and where unanswered questions remain.

**The Mysterious Message**

"Remember the last game?"

A simple message that brings five separated friends together again.

**The Missing Blocks**

Two missing pieces from the original Jenga set become part of the mystery.

**The Final Two Blocks**

"MY BEST FRIEND BETRAYED ME..."

"FORGIVENESS."

Two inscriptions that reveal the emotional heart of the story.
